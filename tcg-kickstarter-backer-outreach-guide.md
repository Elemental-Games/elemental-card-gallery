# Finding TCG Kickstarter Backers & Whales — What Actually Works

**Bottom line:** There is **no public list** of top Kickstarter backers with emails. Kickstarter and BackerKit don't share backer data. But here's what you *can* do.

---

## What Doesn't Exist

- ❌ Public "whale database" of high-value TCG backers
- ❌ Email list of Altered/Sorcery/Rise backers (that data is private to those creators)
- ❌ Kickstarter Super Backer directory with contact info

---

## What You CAN Do

### 1. Spot Super Backers on Comment Sections (Manual)

**Super Backer** = backed 25+ projects with $10+ in the past year. They get a badge next to their name when they comment.

**How:**
1. Go to successful TCG Kickstarter pages: [Altered](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/equinox-studio/altered-tcg), [Rise](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/darkscreen/rise-11), [Sorcery](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/eric-lang/sorcery-contested-realm), [Nostalgix](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/nostalgixtcg/nostalgix-trading-card-game), etc.
2. Open the **Comments** tab — commenters are backers, and Super Backers show a badge
3. Click their username → view their Kickstarter profile
4. When *your* campaign is live, you can message them via Kickstarter (if they've backed or commented on a project)

**Limitation:** You get usernames, not emails. Outreach happens through Kickstarter messaging or by checking if they've linked social/website on their profile.

### 2. Cross-Promotion with Other TCG Creators

Reach their backers *through them*:

- **BackerKit Cross-Collab** — Partner with another TCG creator launching around the same time. Joint reward for backing both. Their backers discover you.
- **Creator swaps** — Trade update mentions, newsletter shoutouts, or social posts with Altered, Rise, Sorcery, Shard Bugs creators.
- **Kickstarter's project discovery** — Get featured in "Projects We Love" or category pages so their backers discover you organically.

**Who to contact:** Equinox (Altered), Dark Screen (Rise), Erik Dahlman/Ctrl+Alt+Elite (Sorcery), Shard Bugs team — their websites/Discords often have creator or business contact info.

### 3. Join TCG Backer Communities (No Emails, But Audience Access)

| Community | How to Reach Their Backers |
|-----------|----------------------------|
| **Altered Discord** | Very active. Join as a creator, participate, share your project when it fits (don't spam). Their backers = TCG enthusiasts. |
| **Sorcery communities** | Reddit r/SorceryTCG, Discord servers — same approach |
| **Rise TCG** | BackerKit/Discord if they have one |
| **TCG Facebook groups** | "TCG Collectors," "Kickstarter Board Games," etc. — share your campaign, engage genuinely |
| **Reddit** | r/TCG, r/kickstarter, r/boardgames — follow sub rules, share when appropriate |

You're not getting emails here; you're putting your project in front of people who back TCGs.

### 4. BackerTracker (BackerKit)

**Use:** [BackerKit BackerTracker](https://www.backerkit.com/) — tracks funding trends, backer counts, pledge levels for live and past campaigns.

**What it gives you:** Market intel (what tiers work, funding velocity), NOT individual backer emails.

**Use it for:** Picking reward tiers, pricing, and launch timing based on similar TCG campaigns.

### 5. Kickstarter Games Team

**Contact:** press@kickstarter.com, or the Games Outreach team (Maggie Clayton, Head of Games).

**Ask:** Feature consideration, "Projects We Love," or inclusion in Games category spotlights. They can surface you to their Games followers and super-backers.

## Recommended Action Plan

1. **This week:** Visit Altered, Sorcery, Rise comment sections. Note 20–50 Super Backer usernames. When your campaign is live, consider a short, personal Kickstarter message (if their profile allows it).
2. **Pre-launch:** Email 2–3 TCG creators (Altered, Rise, etc.) for cross-promo or mutual updates. Many are open to it.
3. **Launch:** Submit to Kickstarter for "Projects We Love" and Games spotlight. Use press@kickstarter.com.
4. **Ongoing:** Be active in TCG Discord servers and Facebook groups. Share your campaign when it’s relevant and within community rules.

---

## Why There’s No "List"

Backer emails and pledge amounts are **creator-only data**. Kickstarter and BackerKit don’t publish or sell this. GDPR, CAN-SPAM, and privacy rules make it risky for creators to share backer data. The only practical options are: work with creators who have that access, or reach TCG enthusiasts through public communities and discovery.
