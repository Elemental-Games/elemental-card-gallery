## UTM convention
Use these keys consistently:
- `utm_source`: platform (tiktok, instagram, meta, youtube, email, discord, lgs)
- `utm_medium`: channel type (organic, paid, referral, newsletter, qr)
- `utm_campaign`: timeboxed campaign (holiday_bundles_2025_12, holiday_bundles_2026_01)
- `utm_content`: creative identifier (openpack_01, founderstory_02, bundlebreakdown_01, retarget_01)
