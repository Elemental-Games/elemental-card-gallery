## Caveats (Important)

1. **Conversion:** BackerKit and other experts say purchased lists often convert at ~0% vs 1–5% for organic lists.
2. **Compliance:** CAN-SPAM (US) and GDPR (EU) still apply. Include physical address, honor opt-outs, and avoid misleading subject lines.
3. **Quality:** "Confirmed backer" claims are hard to verify. Many lists are built from opt-ins, forums, surveys—not directly from Kickstarter’s backer data.
4. **Best practice:** Organic list-building (teaser page, cross-promo, communities) usually performs better long-term.
