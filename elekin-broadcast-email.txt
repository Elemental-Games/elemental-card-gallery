Elekin: Masters of Kinbrold is live on Kickstarter. It's a strategic TCG built for ~30-minute matches with a few things we're proud of:

• **Resources** — 4-element Essence system (Air, Water, Fire, Earth). Generate from creatures, spend to summon and activate abilities. Carries over turn to turn.

• **Combat** — Strength/Agility system where Agility sets strike order. Higher agility hits first—tactical, not just raw stats. Block, dodge, and counter-attack.

• **Comebacks** — 3-tier Shield system that powers up as it breaks. Games stay tense even when someone gets ahead.

• **Cards** — Every card has a QR code linking to stats, lore, and deck-building notes.

Low barrier to entry, strategic depth, no 3-hour marathons. We're already in stores and expanding.
