## Questions to Ask Potential Partners

1. **Portfolio/Experience:**
   - "Can you share examples of product/campaign videos you've created?"
   - "What's your preferred style/approach?"

2. **Availability:**
   - "What's your timeline/availability?"
   - "How many revisions do you typically include?"

3. **Compensation:**
   - "Which compensation structure works best for you?"
   - "For revenue share, what would make you comfortable with that model?"

4. **Process:**
   - "What assets/information do you need from me?"
   - "How do you prefer to collaborate? (Scripts, storyboards, etc.)"

5. **Commitment:**
   - "Are you interested in ongoing work post-Kickstarter?"
   - "What happens if the campaign doesn't fund? (for revenue share model)"

---

## Red Flags to Watch For

❌ Asks for money upfront before showing portfolio  
❌ No examples of similar work  
❌ Unrealistic promises ("guaranteed viral")  
❌ Doesn't ask questions about the project  
❌ Unresponsive/delayed communication  

✅ **Green Flags:**
✅ Shows enthusiasm about the project  
✅ Asks thoughtful questions  
✅ Provides portfolio with similar work  
✅ Clear communication about process/timeline  
✅ Open to discussing compensation structure
