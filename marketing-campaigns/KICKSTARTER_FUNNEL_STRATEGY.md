## 🚨 THE CRITICAL QUESTION: What Should Visitors Do?

### **PRIMARY GOAL: Email Signups for Kickstarter Notifications** ⭐
**Why this is #1:**
- **Email list = Day 1 backers** (Kickstarter's algorithm rewards fast funding)
- **30% funded in first 48 hours** = social proof that drives more backers
- **Pre-launch email list** is the #1 predictor of Kickstarter success
- **You can always sell products later**, but you can't recreate launch momentum

### **SECONDARY GOAL: Pre-Launch Product Sales** (If inventory available)
**Why this helps:**
- Generates immediate revenue
- Creates social proof (existing customers)
- Validates product-market fit
- **BUT:** Don't let it distract from Kickstarter prep

### **TERTIARY GOAL: Education & Engagement**
**Why this matters:**
- Builds trust and interest
- Creates community
- **BUT:** Must lead to email signup or purchase

## 🔄 THE OPTIMAL VISITOR JOURNEY

### **Path 1: New Visitor (Cold Traffic)**
1. **Lands on site** → Sees "Kickstarter Launch Feb 17" hero
2. **Scrolls down** → Sees email capture form with value prop
3. **Exit intent** → Popup: "Don't miss launch - Get notified"
4. **Signs up** → Welcome email with Kickstarter preview
5. **Returns** → Sees countdown + Kickstarter preview
6. **Launch day** → Gets email → Backs on Kickstarter

### **Path 2: Returning Visitor (Warm Traffic)**
1. **Returns to site** → Sees countdown timer
2. **Clicks "Learn More"** → Goes to Kickstarter preview page
3. **Sees value** → Signs up for email (if not already)
4. **Launch day** → Gets email → Backs on Kickstarter

### **Path 3: Ready to Buy (Hot Traffic)**
1. **Lands on site** → Sees "Shop Now" option
2. **Buys product** → Gets email confirmation
3. **Email sequence** → "Thanks for buying! Want early access to Kickstarter?"
4. **Launch day** → Gets email → Backs on Kickstarter (upsell)
